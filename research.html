Cognitive Achievement Distributions and Adult Wage Returns: Evidence from NELS and ELS

Summer 2026 · R, Stata

Public attention has centered on falling national test scores for students since the 2020 pandemic. In fact, scores for the bottom half of students have been dropping since the mid-2010s (NCES 2024). I study the NELS and ELS cohorts: 10th graders in 1990 and 2002, finding that the relationship between test scores and adult wages strengthened significantly over time for women, with no evidence of decreases for men. Through a statistical decomposition of these estimates, the growth in returns to women’s test scores appears to be primarily driven by increased wage returns for the bottom half of scorers. These results highlight the need for additional research on low-scoring students, whose recent declines in test scores threaten to widen disparities in both cognitive achievement and labor-market outcomes.
